## C) Level & Strategy

**Detected level:** Mid-Senior IC ("3–5 years, depth valued over tenure") — this is a Senior SDE / SDE2.5 equivalent with strong autonomy expectations.

**Stevens' natural level:** SDE2 with ~5 years — squarely in range. The Ackathon build (zero-to-one in a sprint) and the Admissibility AI Agent (production agentic system) are exactly the evidence they want.

**"Sell senior without lying" plan:**
- Lead with the Admissibility AI Agent as the primary proof of production AI ownership: "I architected an agentic claims classification system that auto-analyses claim parameters against configurable rules and cross-references damage reports with incident descriptions using ML — deployed to production at Acko."
- RSA metrics (+91% registrations) prove the "move fast + measure" instinct they want.
- Ackathon build proves the "zero to demo in under two weeks" speed directly.
- Dell patent on Gen AI for Code Reusability shows breadth beyond pure implementation.
- Frame as: "I ship AI systems end-to-end — from problem scoping to prod monitoring — not just feature work."

**Application note:** This role requires a 300–500 word written narrative (most technically complex system built). Prepare this carefully — the Admissibility AI Agent is the right subject: problem, architecture decisions, a production failure/fix, and retrospective improvement.

**If downleveled:** Role is IC-focused so downleveling risk is low. If comp is below ₹35L, flag and negotiate.
