## C) Level & Strategy

**Detected level:** Senior (6+ years, 2+ LLM production) — Stevens is slightly below on total years (~5yr) but has strong AI production proof points.

**Strategy — "depth over duration":**
- The 6+ year requirement is a guideline, not a hard filter. 5 years with two production AI systems (Admissibility Agent + Global Care LLM pipeline) is a stronger signal than 6 years of generic backend work.
- Lead with the AI production track: Dell Speech Recognition (85% accuracy custom model), Rasa NLP chatbot, Admissibility AI Agent, Acko Global Care LLM pipeline. That's a clear progression of AI system complexity over 5 years.
- Frame the experience gap as: "I optimise for impact per year, not years per role."

**Python positioning:**
- Python appears in AI/ML work, the PRISMO chatbot, and the energy prediction model. In interviews, be ready to write Python fluently. If there's a take-home, do it in Python.
- Avoid leading with Java/Spring Boot in this context.

**If downleveled to SDE2 equivalent:** Razorpay's SDE2-equivalent at AlphaSense would need comp verification. AlphaSense India comp (₹16–35L range per Glassdoor) is a concern — see Block D.
